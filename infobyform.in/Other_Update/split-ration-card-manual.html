Split Ration Card Manual

Follow the same flow shown in the PDF: open the RC Online portal, login through MeriPehchaan, choose Apply for Split, select members, upload documents, and save the generated Application ID.

Start Steps Open RC Online

Before You Apply

Keep these points ready before starting the split ration card request.

Active member required

The applicant should be an active member of an active ration card.

Verified details

Only verified member names and Aadhaar numbers can be split from the original card.

Adult member rule

After split, at least one adult member must remain on the original ration card.

Steps to Request Split Ration Card Application

The steps below follow the PDF sequence from portal login to final submission.

01

Open RC Online Portal

Go to https://rconline.bihar.gov.in and use the login option on the RC Online site.

  • Click the Login button.
  • The site redirects to the MeriPehchaan Jan Parichay SSO portal.

02

Sign Up or Login Through MeriPehchaan

Use MeriPehchaan according to your user type.

  • Fresh or new users should click Sign up for MeriPehchaan and register with the required fields.
  • Existing users can login with their MeriPehchaan ID.
  • If RCMS login details are already migrated, use the Mobile tab and enter the RCMS login ID and password in place of mobile number and password.

03

Reach the Bihar RCMS Portal

After login, there are two possible redirects.

Scenario A

You are directly redirected to the Bihar RCMS dashboard.

Scenario B

You land on the Jan Parichay dashboard. Choose Bihar State Service from the left menu, then select Bihar Ration Card Online Production.

Access Now

When the popup appears, click Access Now to open the RCMS Bihar portal.

04

Select Apply for Split

On the RCMS dashboard, open the application menu.

  • Choose the Apply menu.
  • Click Apply for Split.
Figures 07-08
05

Enter Ration Card and Applicant Details

The Split Application form appears after choosing Apply for Split.

  • Enter the ration card number in the input box.
  • If the card exists and meets the criteria, details are fetched from the database.
  • Fill the applicant details shown with all active members.
Note: Only active members of the ration card can apply for splitting members from existing cards.
Figures 09-10
06

Select Members for Split

After applicant details are submitted, the active member list appears.

  • Select members for split using the checkbox.
  • Selected members display on the right side.
  • Use Remove to undo a selection.
  • Use Edit to modify allowed member details.
  • Use Add Member to add a new member to this card.
Important: If Aadhaar number and member name are already verified, only basic details can be changed.
Figures 11-14
07

Proceed, Fill Annexure, Upload, and Submit

After selecting members and making any required edits or additions, continue to final submission.

  • Click Proceed to Split.
  • Fill the Annexure accordingly.
  • Click Submit and Go for Upload.
  • Select all required documents and click Upload Document and Final Submission.
  • Save the generated Application ID for future reference.
Figures 15-18

Documents to Upload

In the document upload section, prepare the following items before final submission.

Photo

Applicant/member photo as required by the portal.

Signature

Signature file for the application.

Merged document

A single merged document containing required supporting papers.

Aadhaar numbers

Aadhaar details of all applicable members.

Residential certificate

Residence proof document.

Passbook

Bank passbook document.

Income certificate

Income certificate document.

Caste or disability certificate

Upload if applicable.

Final Check

Confirm member selection, Annexure details, and uploaded documents before final submission. Keep the Application ID safely.
